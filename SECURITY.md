# Security Policy

We take the security of our projects and our users very seriously. All vulnerabilities, no matter how trivial, should be reported.

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability in any C-Tech-Labs repository, please contact us directly at [replace-with-email] and do not open a public issue. Provide as much information as you can, including:

- A description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce or proof of concept
- Any known impacts or potential mitigations

We will acknowledge receipt of your report within one business day and aim to provide a status update within five business days.

## Supported versions

We generally support only the latest major version of our repositories. When in doubt, include version information or commit SHA when reporting.

## Responsibility

After a vulnerability is confirmed, we will work to address it promptly and coordinate disclosure. Please do not discuss or publish details until we have released a fix.

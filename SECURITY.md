# Security Policy

We take the security of our projects and our users seriously. If you believe you have found a vulnerability, please follow the steps below so we can address it quickly and responsibly.

## Reporting a vulnerability
1. **Email us at `security@c-tech-labs.com`.** Do not open a public issue for security findings.
2. Include as much detail as possible:
   - A description of the vulnerability and potential impact
   - Steps to reproduce or proof of concept
   - Affected versions, commit SHAs, or deployments
   - Suggested mitigations, if any
3. We will acknowledge receipt within **one business day** and provide an initial status update within **five business days**.

If you require encrypted communication, request a public key in your initial email.

## Handling and disclosure process
- Reports are triaged for severity and scope.
- We may request additional details or reproduction steps.
- Fixes are developed privately; we avoid disclosing details until a patch is available.
- Once a fix is released, we will publish a summary, remediation steps, and any required migration guidance.
- Credit will be given to reporters upon request unless anonymity is preferred.

## Supported versions
We generally support the latest major release line of each repository. Security fixes may be backported to prior versions at the maintainers’ discretion based on impact and feasibility.

## Safe harbor
We will not pursue legal action for security research conducted in good faith and in accordance with this policy. Avoid privacy violations, service degradation, or data destruction during testing.
